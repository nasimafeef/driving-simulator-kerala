Driving Simulator Kerala Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026 Applies to com.evastudios.vehiclesimulator

At a glance

  • There are no accounts. You never give us an email address, phone number or password.
  • The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo permissions.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics to see which features people actually use.
  • Multiplayer is optional. It is the only part of the app that identifies you to anyone else, and only by a display name you choose.
  • We do not sell your data, and the app currently shows no advertisements.

Who this covers

This policy describes how Nasim Afeef K J (“we”) handles information in the Android game Driving Simulator Kerala, package com.evastudios.vehiclesimulator. It covers the app only, not any website or storefront that lists it.

Usage statistics

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics. It records how the game is used so we can tell which vehicles, control schemes and destinations people prefer, and where players get stuck. These records are tied to a randomly generated app instance identifier, not to you.

Rather than describe this vaguely, here is every event the game sends:

Events sent by the game
EventSent whenDetail included
screen_viewYou open a menu or screenScreen name
button_clickYou tap a menu buttonButton name
vehicle_selectYou choose a vehicleVehicle name
control_modeYou change the steering controlsControl mode
source_selectYou pick a starting pointIn-game place name
destination_selectYou pick a destinationIn-game place name
fell_in_waterYour vehicle enters waterNone

A note on “place”. The places in source_select and destination_select are towns inside the game’s fictional map of Kerala. They are not your real-world location. The app has no access to GPS or any location sensor.

The game also stores two preferences against that anonymous identifier — your most recently used vehicle and your chosen control mode — so we can understand usage patterns across sessions.

Separately, Firebase collects a standard set of technical details on its own: device model, operating system version, app version, language, an approximate region derived from your IP address, and session timing. Google’s handling of this is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security page and the Google Privacy Policy.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer uses Epic Online Services from Epic Games. If you never open the multiplayer menu, none of this applies to you.

How you are signed in

The app signs in anonymously using Epic’s Device ID method. No email address, password, or Epic Games account is involved, and you are never asked to create one. Epic issues an identifier tied to your installation so that you can connect to other players. Removing the app ends that association.

Your display name

You may enter a display name. If you do not, the game assigns one at random, such as Driver4821. This name is stored on your device in the game’s settings file, and:

  • appears above your vehicle to other players in the same session;
  • is listed publicly in the games list while you are hosting a public game.

Because the name is shown to strangers, please do not use your real name or anything else you would not want a stranger to read. You can change it at any time from the multiplayer menu.

Connecting players

Players connect to one another directly. To make that connection, Epic’s service handles the network addresses of the participants, as any online game must. We do not store those addresses. Epic’s handling is described in the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Kept only on your device

Your display name, settings and game progress are saved in the app’s own storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for, or have any way to obtain, your real-world location.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or payment details.
  • We do not sell or rent information to anyone.
  • The app currently contains no advertising and no advertising SDK, so no advertising identifier is collected.

The permissions the app declares are limited to what a networked game needs: internet and network state, keeping the screen awake, vibration, audio settings, and notifications.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the multiplayer display name, contact us at afeef.nasim@gmail.com and we will remove it.

How long information is kept

Analytics records are retained by Google under the retention setting on our Firebase project and are deleted automatically when that period elapses. Multiplayer sessions exist only while they are being played; once a session ends, its listing and its display names are gone.

Your choices

  • Skip multiplayer. Playing single player sends nothing that identifies you to other people.
  • Change your display name at any time from the multiplayer menu.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your device and end the Epic device association.
  • Ask us. Write to afeef.nasim@gmail.com to request deletion of analytics data associated with your installation. Please include the approximate date and time you first installed the game, as that is the only way we can identify anonymous records.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects, we will update this page and move the effective date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: afeef.nasim@gmail.com.
